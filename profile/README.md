# Keel Stats

Advanced stats for the NBA and NFL: live scores, box scores, play by play, our own player ratings (Keel Impact,
RAPM, EPA), game projections, injury pages and player-props research tools. In six languages: English, Spanish,
Portuguese, German, French and Ukrainian. We are a small team from Ukraine. Keel Stats is a research tool: we show
the numbers, we take no bets and give no picks.
